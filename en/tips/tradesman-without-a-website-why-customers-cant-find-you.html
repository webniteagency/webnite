Tradesman without a website? Why customers can't find you — and how to fix it

Webnite Team Last updated: June 2026 3 min read

Short answer

When a pipe bursts, someone grabs their phone and types 'plumber Brežice'. If you don't have a website (or at least Google profile), Google doesn't show you — and the customer calls the competition. Solution: €500–€800 for a solid single-page site + free Google Business Profile.

For a tradesman in a smaller town (Posavje, Štajerska, Dolenjska) who mostly works through referrals — wondering if they even need a website.

Why referrals are no longer enough

Just 10 years ago a tradesman lived from referrals — neighbor recommended to neighbor, that one to relative. Now it happens differently: the neighbor sends a Google link where the plumber is already shown with reviews, photos of work, phone to call.

Without this you're invisible. Even if someone refers you, the customer first checks Google. If you're not there — or you're there with one incomplete mention — they often choose competition that's 'more serious'.

Minimum you need (and it's achievable)

Google Business Profile (free). This comes first. Create a profile, upload 5–10 photos of your work, add opening hours, phone, address. Give customers a link to review you. This is the most important free step that 80% of Slovenian tradesmen get wrong.

Simple website (€500–€800). You don't need a store, you don't need 10 pages. One long page that says: what you do, in which area, your references (photos), contact. This covers 90% of a tradesman's needs.

Google My Business + basic SEO (included in the site). So Google finds you when someone nearby types your service.

Real example — carpenter from Krško valley

A carpenter (Tomaž, 47) worked through referrals for 20 years. In 2025 he had a slower season — orders dropped. He invested €650 in a simple site and €0 in a Google profile (made it himself).

After 3 months: the site brought 12 new inquiries from previously unknown areas (Brežice, Sevnica, Ljubljana). One of them was a kitchen for €4,500. Investment recovered 7×.

Key point: the site didn't have to be perfect, just solidly made with the right text — what he does, where, and how to reach him.

Typical tradesman approach — what to do in order

Step Price Time
1. Google Business Profile Free 1 hour
2. 5–10 work photos (with phone) Free 1 day
3. Ask 5 old customers for Google review Free 1 week
4. Simple website (landing page) €500–€800 1 week
5. Basic Google Ads (optional) €150 + ads After launch
Watch out for

'First I'll do Facebook, website later.' Understandable — Facebook is free. Problem: Facebook closes a business, the profile disappears. The website is yours and permanent. Start with a basic site, add Facebook next to it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I really need a site if I have Facebook?

Yes, because Facebook isn't in Google search for professional services. 'Plumber near me' Google search without a site and Google profile doesn't find you.

How much does monthly maintenance cost?

For a simple tradesman site €10–€25 per month (hosting + domain). Agency maintenance contract usually not needed at this simple level.

Can I make the site myself (Wix, WordPress)?

You can, but the result is often visibly amateurish — bad photos, bad fonts, bad text. €500 with a professional often looks 10× better than €0 yourself.

How long until Google shows the site?

After publishing: 1–4 weeks for Google to index pages and show them in results. With a good Google profile (free) you're visible immediately.
