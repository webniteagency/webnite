Google or Meta Ads: which are right for my business?

Webnite Team Last updated: June 2026 3 min read

Short answer

Google Ads: for people who are ACTIVELY searching for your product/service (e.g. 'plumber Ljubljana'). Meta Ads (Instagram, Facebook): for people who don't know they need you — they discover you while browsing (clothing, products, events).

For a business owner who has never advertised, has €300–€1,000 monthly budget and wonders where to start.

The difference in one sentence

Google = I catch existing demand. Someone types 'pizza delivery Maribor' — your ad appears right to them.

Meta = I create demand that doesn't exist yet. Someone browses Instagram and sees your nice dress — they think about buying it.

When to choose Google Ads

Google is right if you have a service or product people actively search for, especially locally:

  • Tradesman (plumber, electrician, locksmith) — when a pipe bursts, people immediately search Google.
  • Service activities (hairdresser, beautician, gym, tailoring) — searching near home.
  • B2B and specialized services — where the buyer knows what they're looking for.
  • Local restaurants, bars, stores.

When to choose Meta Ads (Instagram, Facebook)

Meta is right when products are discovered while browsing — visually attractive, lifestyle, fashion:

  • Clothing, fashion accessories, cosmetics, jewelry.
  • Handmade products, local specialties.
  • Events, concerts, performances.
  • Online stores with image catalog.

How much budget you need to start

Google Ads: €200–€500 per month for first test (click costs €0.30–€2 depending on industry). At €1,000/month you can see real results.

Meta Ads: €150–€300 for first test, then scaling based on results. Meta allows smaller starting budgets than Google.

Important: advertising budget is separate from agency fee for management. The agency typically takes a monthly fee of €200–€800, while you pay the ad budget directly to Google/Meta.

Common mistakes

'I'll do both and see which works.' Bad idea on a €300 budget. Split €150 + €150 is too little for meaningful testing. Choose one platform based on your activity, give it 1–2 months, then add the other.

Frequently asked questions

Do Google and Meta complement each other?

Yes, they often work best together — Meta builds awareness, Google catches when people search later. But don't start with both; start with one, the other comes later.

How long until I see results?

First data in 1–2 weeks, truly optimized campaigns after 1–3 months. Both platform algorithms need time and data to learn.

Do I need an agency or can I do it myself?

You can DIY for very small budgets (under €200/mo) and simple industries. At €500+ monthly an agency usually pays off, because a well-optimized ad saves more than the fee costs.

What if I have a very small budget (€50/mo)?

At €50, advertising is often ineffective. Better to invest in solid organic Instagram/Facebook posts (free) or Google Business Profile for local businesses.
